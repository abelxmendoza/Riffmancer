Instrument samples bundled with Riffmancer

Notes C and Gb in octaves 1-6 from the FluidR3_GM General MIDI SoundFont (by Frank Wen),
as rendered by Benjamin Gleitzman's midi-js-soundfonts (MIT license):
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts  (FluidR3_GM is distributed under the MIT license)

Every other General MIDI instrument is loaded on demand from
https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/

Files were made mono and re-encoded smaller for the browser.
