Drum samples bundled with Riffmancer

acoustic/  Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Sam Gossner and contributors.
           License: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

tr808/     Roland TR-808 sample set 1.0.0 by Michael Fischer (Technopolis, 1994),
           released by the author as free samples. Source: https://github.com/danigb/samples
           (audio/drum-machines/TR-808, which includes the original TR808.TXT)

All files were trimmed, made mono and level-matched for the browser.
