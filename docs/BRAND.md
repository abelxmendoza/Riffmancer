# Riffmancer visual identity

The canonical palette is **Toxic Signal**:

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#080E0D` | App canvas and transport wells |
| Surface | `#14201B` | Panels, cards, and track surfaces |
| Primary | `#A3E635` | Primary actions, active states, and waveform progress |
| Secondary | `#22C55E` | Hover states, status indicators, and waveform context |
| Text | `#E5F7E7` | Headings and body copy, with opacity for muted text |

The logo direction is a skull constructed from musical notation. It uses the dark background with primary and secondary accents; it must not contain purple, white logo fills, or alternate product names.

- `frontend/public/riffmancer-mark.svg` is the simplified header and favicon mark.
- `frontend/public/riffmancer-emblem.png` is the full-resolution branded emblem for sharing and large-format use.
